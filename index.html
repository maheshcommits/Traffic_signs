    Project sheet

    Abstract

    This project proposes a CNN classifier to classify road traffic signs from dashcam images, addressing the problem of traffic sign recognition through a data-driven approach. Data collected from the GTSRB traffic-sign dataset is cleaned, transformed, and used to build and evaluate the underlying model. The outcome is delivered as an ADAS driver-assist module, enabling practical adoption beyond the classroom.

    What this prototype is

    The drive is simulated and the sign position comes from the simulator. The recogniser here is a template-matching stand-in that takes the same 32 x 32 crop and returns class probabilities, covering 14 of the 43 GTSRB classes. Once the CNN above is trained on GTSRB and exported, it replaces the classify step and the rest of the page stays the same.

    Prototype for academic demonstration. Alerts are advisory and not a substitute for driver judgement.
